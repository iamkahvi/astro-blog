## law school application

I’ve been working on my personal statement. It’s good because it’s forcing me to contend with why I actually want to go to law school.

Do I want to become a lawyer? No.

Do I want to change the system? Not really.

Am I interested in it? Yes.

Do I want to learn more about the law? Yes.

I’m trying to be honest with myself about it. It’s kind of devolved into me justifying *why* it’s interesting to me, which is a mystery. Why why why why why.
