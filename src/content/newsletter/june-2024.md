Here’s my review of the concert:

> **Hisaishi Conducts Hisaishi**
> 
> You gotta eat your vegetables for this one
> 
> **“Metaphysica”**
> 
> 1- Very postmodern. *Everything* is deconstructed but especially the percussion. It’s off on purpose? Or I’m too dumb to understand the tempo. At the same time, it’s very precise, especially with the snare. It has to be, because there’s so much crazy shit going on.
> 
> 2- More flowy, a melody/phrase that gets deconstructed. Reminiscent of modulation pitch and frequency on a synthesizer. I could notice the same order of notes resurfacing.
> 
> **No** **applause break**. Heard two claps in the pause then they started back up again.
> 
> 3- He's certainly using all the musicians. Even if they are playing out of sync. *A lot* of dissonance - not great tbh
> 
> To me, metaphysica is not supposed to be structured or linear or cohesive. It’s meant to test the boundaries of the medium first, and producing an enjoyable tune second.
> 
> Not a bad thing to explore that way. I’d just rather not sit through it!!!
> 
> **La valse**
> 
> 1- A waltz! Not quite normal obviously but something close to it. Was not dissonant!! I liked it!
> 
> **“Spirited Away” Suite**
> 
> 😮. Floored.
> 
> The encore almost brought tears to my eyes! Moist eyes at least.

But I actually wasn’t in Toronto to watch the Symphony. I was there for Shopify Summit. It was a blast!
