## learnings

#### north should be up

I get a much better sense of direction when I configure my Google maps to have a fixed orientation (so north is always up):

![](https://cdn.kahvipatel.com/newsletter-assets/174144127-img-009.png)

Even though it’s not always pointed in the direction I'm going, it gives me a much better idea of where I am on the island.

#### what I’m eating

I’ve realized that my intuition for how much I'm eating is widely inaccurate. The gap between how much I think I’ve eaten and reality is huge!

This revelation has come after tracking my macros for a couple weeks (with [this app](https://macrofactorapp.com/macrofactor/)). More on that next month!
