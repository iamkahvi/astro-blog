## hotel laundry, how to

I was living in a hotel in Toronto for a while in July and had to figure out how to do laundry.

- The hotel charged $50 per dress shirt and didn’t seem to have pricing for anything non-formal.
- The closest laundromat was 30 minutes away and I didn’t have time to do that anyways.
- There were several dry cleaning places around, but the turnaround time was like 4 days which made no sense for me.

This is a long build up to me admitting that *I did my laundry in the hotel sink with shower gel and shampoo* (a first for me).

It actually worked quite well for the shirts and underwear I had - but the socks wouldn’t dry.

## ways to dry socks in a hotel, ranked

1. hair dryer
2. compress them between two towels and put some weight on it
3. hang them to dry

I, of course, had to try methods 2 and 3 before noticing the hair dryer. With that thing my socks got dry in about 5 minutes.

*When drying socks in a hotel, use the hair dryer.*
