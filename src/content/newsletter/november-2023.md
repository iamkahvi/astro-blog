## mexico

In an annual effort to escape the god-awful dreariness of Victoria, I was in Mexico for most of November.

This trip convinced me that the best way to travel is to stay as close as possible to a park. We stayed next to [Plaza Río de Janeiro](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Lo7x4NSGeF3xsCi39) in [Colonia Roma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colonia_Roma). It was lovely. My favourite food was at Taqueria Orinoco and Taquería Álvaro Obregón. Here’s a little video of the trip!

## spanish

On a related note, I want to learn how to speak Spanish.

It’s become clear to me that to maximize my experience in Mexico, I need to speak and understand the language. So far my knowledge is quite limited.

I did start watching Spanish tv with the subtitles on. It’s quite helpful for getting used to the tone and cadence. I can recommend [Elite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elite_\(TV_series\)), it feels like Riverdale but things are slightly less unhinged.

I kept a note of common phrases because I left my Spanish phrasebook on the plane:

![](https://cdn.kahvipatel.com/newsletter-assets/139766578-img-001.png)

Aside from the practical merits, I enjoy the tone and cadence of Spanish. It sounds smooth and soft and flowy. English is more jagged and sharp. I want to sink into that other tone more.
