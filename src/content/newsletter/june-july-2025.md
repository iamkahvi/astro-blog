### journaling

I’ve been writing **one sentence everyday** since February 6th.

As a result, I have a pretty accurate description of what I was doing on any given day this year. Useful if I’m ever tried in court and need an alibi.

An entry is something like:

> 2025.06.17 - Tuesday
> 
> First day back was intense. Many tasks. But a beautiful bike ride with folks after work :)

This is unstructured — my programmer brain would like something like this:

> date: 2025.06.17  
> location: Victoria  
> activities: bike ride  
> people: Ashley, Alex, Julia

That way, I could write a program to extract cumulative statistics from my entries.

E.g.

![](https://cdn.kahvipatel.com/newsletter-assets/168533409-img-001.jpg)

Still, GPT could *probably* do this with unstructured (normal) entries.

Then, I could ask it questions like:

- Was I injured or sick in the past month?
- What dates was I in Montreal?
- Who did we have dinner with on that Tuesday in April?

You may be saying:

> *Kahvi, that’s what having a memory is!! Just remember things!*

### how to be more engaging on the internet

Lately, I’ve noticed how I engage more people online. It falls on two axes: how much content I put out and how personal it is.

![](https://cdn.kahvipatel.com/newsletter-assets/168533409-img-005.png)

#### content volume + medium

The more bytes I upload, the more people engage.

This is kind of obvious. A picture is worth a thousand words, a video is a thousand pictures, etc.

Reading a newsletter is *harder* than watching a Twitch stream.

So, in my opinion, the more bytes uploaded, the more attention and engagement.

#### personal expression

The more personal/open I am, the more people engage.

Things like showing more parts of my life and the people around me, being more honest and vulnerable, etc.

**Sidenote:**

> It’s hard to know if this pattern is a cause or effect. As in — are you more popular online because you’re open? Or are you open because you’re more popular? Because more people will engage?

#### self-evaluation

I acknowledge that this newsletter is usually weak on both of those axes. My expression is neither visually intense nor that personally vulnerable.

**I’m not sure yet if I want that to change.** But it does make me think about starting a vlog again!
