## Xmas break

It’s been a break about nose-breathing.

After reading the first few chapters of [this book](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Breath:_The_New_Science_of_a_Lost_Art) (that I did not finish) and absorbing its propaganda, I’m intent on spending less time breathing through my mouth.

The convincing parts weren’t the medical studies referenced (sort of because the author isn’t a doctor and it felt a bit preachy).

What resonated with me was the history of breath. The practice of inhaling and exhaling *deeply* and *slowly* is eerily universal. It comes up in eastern meditation, Christian prayer, aboriginal tribes, even the Navy SEALs do some version of it. About [6 seconds in and 6 seconds out](https://www.vice.com/en/article/kzxe83/this-breathing-exercise-can-calm-you-down-in-a-few-minutes). All through the nose.

I’m not sure if this is actually making me healthier, but it does make me *feel* *better*. And I’ll settle for the placebo.
