## gridcalendar.ca

During the trip I started updating [last year](https://www.newsletter.kahvipatel.com/i/154719402/calendar) ’s calendar. In the middle of fiddling with Adobe Illustrator, I stumbled onto [this calendar](https://neatnik.net/calendar/).

It got me interested in the idea of making the calendar open source…

Anyways, that’s [what I did](https://github.com/iamkahvi/gridcal)! and now it’s free to print 😁

I’ve put it up at [gridcalendar.ca](https://gridcalendar.ca/). If you’re interested, go to [gridcalendar.ca](https://gridcalendar.ca/) and follow the instructions (you can [change the colours](https://gridcalendar.ca/configure) too!).
